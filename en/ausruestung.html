Equipment · New Horizons Observatory

What the images are made with

What my images are made with: the observatory in Münster and its equipment. The focus is on high-end optics, a sensitive full-frame camera and very narrow filters – so that fine structure shows up even under a light-polluted suburban sky.

Imaging setup (current)

TelescopeAstro-Physics 130 GTX (Starfire Gran Turismo), 130 mm apochromatic triplet
CorrectorAstro-Physics 0.79× Tri-TCC – f/5, approx. 650 mm focal length; also the 3.5″ Prime Focus Field Flattener (13035FF-2)
CameraZWO ASI6200MM Pro – full-frame sensor (36 × 24 mm), cooled, monochrome
FiltersChroma 3 nm Ha, OIII, SII and Chroma L, R, G, B (50 mm round)
Filter wheelZWO EFW 7 × 2″
FocuserMoonlite NiteCrawler WR35
TiltWanderer ETA M54 (electronic tilt adapter, for all future images)

Second telescope

TelescopeTS Photoline 130 with Riccardi flattener
FocuserMoonlite NiteCrawler WR35
TiltASG EAT “Electronic Photon Cage” (electronic tilt; used for all images so far)
UseMy earlier images were taken with it. Planned as part of a future dual setup. Images using both telescopes list both.
