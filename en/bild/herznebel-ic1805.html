The Heart Nebula

IC 1805 · Melotte 15 · SHO + RGB stars · 2021–2022

Click the image for the full-size view.

The Heart Nebula in Cassiopeia was one of my first big narrowband projects – and one that stretched over almost two years. I used the TS 130 in two configurations, with reducer and with flattener; in total the telescope spent 8 nights on IC 1805.

At its centre sits the young star cluster Melotte 15. Its hot, massive stars are only a few million years old. Their radiation and stellar winds have blown out the huge cavity and sculpted the pillars and arcs along its rim. At the upper right glows IC 1795 with NGC 896, the brightest part of the complex – the so-called Fish Head Nebula.

Hydrogen, oxygen and sulphur each go into their own colour channel (SHO, the “Hubble palette”). Golden tones mainly show hydrogen and sulphur, blue areas ionised oxygen close to the hot stars. The stars come from short RGB exposures so that they keep their natural colours.

This image also has a very personal meaning for me: I dedicated it to my wife and showed it to all our guests at our wedding.
